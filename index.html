About

World Population is an interactive data visualization tracing global population dynamics from 1950 to 2100. Powered by UN World Population Prospects 2024, and the World Bank data. Featuring a fluid design where each particle represents 500,000 people, the application seamlessly transitions across a 3D globe, geographic maps, cluster views, and comparative charts to reveal the core forces shaping human history: growth, migration, aging, and decline.
