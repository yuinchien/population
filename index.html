An interactive data visualization tracing global population dynamics from 1950 to 2100. Featuring a fluid, particle-based design, the application seamlessly transitions across a 3D globe, geographic maps, cluster views, and comparative charts to reveal the core forces shaping human history: growth, migration, aging, and decline. A unified timeline anchors the experience, allowing users to drill down into country, regional, and income-group metrics to surface critical milestones and long-term demographic transitions.

Powered by UN World Population Prospects 2024, and the World Bank data. This is a super fun collaboration with coding agents—Claude and ChatGPT. Learn more.
